MEET YOUR THERAPIST

Dr. Patrice Barnes-Tracey

PhD, LCSW, LMSW  ·  Owner & Founder

Widely recognized for her innovative and integrative approach to social work practice — bridging theory, research, and compassionate clinical care.

Dr. Patrice Barnes-Tracey earned both her Master's degree and Ph.D. from the Graduate School of Social Service at Fordham University. Her scholarly work has made meaningful contributions to the advancement of child welfare practice, with particular emphasis on improving outcomes for children and families impacted by complex trauma, systemic inequities, and mental health challenges.

Dr. Barnes-Tracey is an expert in child welfare practice and mental health, with a career spanning direct service, leadership, clinical practice, and higher education. Her professional journey includes extensive tenure within public and nonprofit social service systems across Connecticut and New York. Her clinical focus includes trauma-informed care, mindfulness-based interventions, and evidence-informed approaches that promote healing, resilience, and emotional regulation.

She is also an adjunct lecturer at several colleges and universities throughout New York and Connecticut, a professional speaker, and a dedicated mentor to both emerging and seasoned social workers.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Our Approach to Healing & Growth

At Noble Rose Counseling & Wellness, we believe that every individual and family has the capacity to heal, grow, and thrive. Our approach is compassionate, culturally responsive, and tailored to meet the unique needs of children, adolescents, adults and families.

We use evidence-based and holistic treatment modalities to support emotional wellness, strengthen relationships, and build lifelong coping skills.

Our Treatment Modalities

TF-CBT

Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

An evidence-based approach designed to help children, adolescents, and families overcome the impact of trauma by addressing distressing thoughts, emotions, and experiences in a safe and structured way.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

CBT helps individuals understand how thoughts, feelings, and behaviors are connected. Clients learn practical tools to manage anxiety, depression, and life stressors by changing unhelpful thinking patterns.

DBT

Dialectical Behavior Therapy

DBT focuses on building skills in emotional regulation, distress tolerance, mindfulness, and healthy relationships. It is especially helpful for individuals who experience intense emotions.

SFBT

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

A goal-oriented approach that focuses on solutions rather than problems. SFBT helps clients identify strengths and create practical steps toward achieving their desired outcomes.

ABT

Attachment-Based Therapy

This approach focuses on strengthening emotional bonds, particularly within families. It is especially beneficial for children and caregivers building trust, security, and healthy relationships.

MBT

Mindfulness-Based Therapy

Incorporates mindfulness practices to help clients stay present, reduce stress, and improve emotional awareness — supporting overall mental wellness and resilience.

MI

Motivational Interviewing

A collaborative, client-centered approach that helps individuals explore and resolve ambivalence about change. MI is especially effective for building motivation and confidence.

SBT

Strengths-Based Therapy

This approach focuses on identifying and building upon each client's strengths, resilience, and inner resources to foster empowerment and growth.

PSC

Psychoeducation & Skills Coaching

We provide education and practical tools to help clients and families better understand mental health, improve communication, and develop effective coping and parenting strategies.
