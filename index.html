Dr. Patrice Barnes-Tracey

PhD, LCSW, LMSW  ·  Founder & CEO

Dr. Patrice Barnes-Tracey brings a depth of knowledge and compassion to her work, with a strong commitment to supporting children, individuals, and families navigating complex life challenges.

With extensive experience in child welfare and mental health, Dr. Barnes-Tracey has worked across direct service, clinical practice, leadership, and higher education settings throughout Connecticut and New York. OUR PHILOSOPHY

Our Approach to Healing & Growth

At Noble Rose Counseling & Wellness, we believe that every individual and family has the capacity to heal, grow, and thrive. Our approach is compassionate, culturally responsive, and tailored to meet the unique needs of children, adolescents, adults and families.

We use evidence-based and holistic treatment modalities to support emotional wellness, strengthen relationships, and build lifelong coping skills.

Our Treatment Modalities

An evidence-based approach designed to help children, adolescents, and families overcome the impact of trauma by addressing distressing thoughts, emotions, and experiences in a safe and structured way.

CBT helps individuals understand how thoughts, feelings, and behaviors are connected. Clients learn practical tools to manage anxiety, depression, and life stressors by changing unhelpful thinking patterns.

DBT focuses on building skills in emotional regulation, distress tolerance, mindfulness, and healthy relationships. It is especially helpful for individuals who experience intense emotions.

A goal-oriented approach that focuses on solutions rather than problems. SFBT helps clients identify strengths and create practical steps toward achieving their desired outcomes.

Incorporates mindfulness practices to help clients stay present, reduce stress, and improve emotional awareness — supporting overall mental wellness and resilience.

Narrative Therapy helps individuals separate themselves from their problems and reframe their personal stories in a more empowering way. Clients are encouraged to explore their experiences, identify their strengths, and rewrite narratives that support healing, growth, and a stronger sense of identity.

Christian Counseling integrates faith-based principles with evidence-based therapeutic practices. This approach supports clients who desire to incorporate their spiritual beliefs into the healing process, using prayer, scripture, and biblical wisdom to promote hope, guidance, and emotional well-being.

WHO WE ARE

Our Core Values

At Noble Rose Counseling & Wellness, our core values guide everything we do. They reflect who we are, how we work, and the experience we strive to create for every client and family.

Compassion

We approach each individual and family with empathy, understanding, and kindness, creating a safe space for healing and growth.

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, and professionalism in all interactions, fostering trust and accountability.

Empowerment

We believe in nurturing each client's strengths and resilience, helping them build confidence and take charge of their healing journey.
