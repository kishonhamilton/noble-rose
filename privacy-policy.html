Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 17, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Noble Rose Counseling & Wellness website at noblerosellc.com. It does not govern clinical records or Protected Health Information (PHI), which are subject to separate HIPAA-compliant policies provided at the time of service.

1. Who We Are

Noble Rose Counseling & Wellness LLC ("Noble Rose," "we," "us," or "our") is a counseling and mental wellness practice led by Dr. Patrice Barnes-Tracey, PhD, LCSW, LMSW, serving clients in New York and Connecticut. Our website address is noblerosellc.com.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: info@noblerosellc.com

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email address, phone number (optional), service of interest, and any message you choose to send.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on the site, browser type, and general location (country/region), collected through analytics tools if enabled.

We do not collect sensitive health information, insurance details, or payment information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or consultation request
  • Follow up regarding services that may be relevant to your needs
  • Communicate important information about our practice

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Contact form submissions are transmitted via Formspree, a secure third-party form processing service, and delivered to our private email. Formspree's privacy practices are available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to avoid sharing sensitive personal or health information through the website contact form.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with the site. Analytics data is collected in aggregate and is not linked to personally identifiable information.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to use this website.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our website, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for any communication from us at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@noblerosellc.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

  • Email: info@noblerosellc.com
  • Service area: New York & Connecticut
